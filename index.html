Simple Useful Apps

Simple, focused, free.

This is a small series of apps for iPhone and Mac. Each one does a single useful thing, and each is free in every sense: free on the App Store, free of subscriptions, free of ads, and open source under a permissive license.

The apps

  • The Old Pod A throwback to a more simple time, this is an iTunes style app for macOs and iPhone with no cloud other than iCloud.
  • Voice Takes Coming Soon! A camera for your voice. Quick dictations. Dictation processing happens all on your own device.
  • Chatfolio Coming Soon! Have a conversation with you ebooks even when you don't have connectivity.

The goal

  • One useful thing. Each app does one thing well and does not aspire to more. There is no roadmap to somewhere else.
  • Free in all the ways. Free to download, no subscription, no ads, no analytics. Nothing is required beyond a normal Apple account and basic iCloud. The source is open under a permissive license.

Support

  • Contribute on GitHub Bug reports, fixes, and improvements--even new apps--are welcome.
