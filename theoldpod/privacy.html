Privacy Policy

The Old Pod · last updated October 2026

The Old Pod collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs, and no server of its own. The app does not send your library, listening history, or preferences to the developer.

Your music and audiobook library is a folder of files on your own device and in your own iCloud Drive. The app reads and plays those files locally. If you use iCloud, syncing those files between your devices is handled entirely by Apple's iCloud Drive under Apple's privacy policy; the developer has no access to your iCloud content.

The Mac app includes FFmpeg for local audio conversion and metadata editing. It does not send your files to a service. Audiobook listening progress and app preferences stay on your device.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to sell, share, or delete on our end, and there are no data-retention or access requests to make.

If you contact support by email, the developer receives the message and information you choose to include and uses it to respond. Support email is separate from the app's operation.
