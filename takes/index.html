Takes

A camera for your voice.

Open Takes, tap the shutter, and speak. Your words appear as you say them. Stop when you are done and the take is ready to copy or share.

Takes is built for the thought you need to catch before it gets away. It does not try to become a writing app, a document system, or a social network. It captures your words and gets out of the way.

How it works

  • Capture first. The app opens straight to the shutter, with the transcript as the viewfinder.
  • Choose the treatment. Keep every word verbatim, lightly clean it up, or turn it into readable paragraphs and lists.
  • Use it anywhere. Copy the current version or send it through the system share sheet.

What it does

  • Live speech-to-text on iPhone
  • Generated titles and a searchable library
  • Verbatim, Light, and Formatted styles
  • Personal dictionary for names and uncommon words
  • Quick launch from supported system controls, Siri, Spotlight, and Shortcuts
  • No account, ads, analytics, or app-owned server

A saved take is text, not an audio archive. Captured audio is used temporarily while the transcript is completed, then removed.
