Help with Voice Takes.

Recording does not start

Open Settings on your iPhone and confirm that Voice Takes has permission to use the microphone. Speech-recognition permission enables the optional words-as-you-speak preview, but durable recording and final file transcription do not depend on that preview. The first high-accuracy transcription may need to download a Whisper model; the Apple fallback may also need a language asset.

A transcript is marked provisional

This means the complete-file transcription did not finish and Voice Takes saved its live preview instead of pretending it was final. The protected recovery audio is still on the phone. Retry transcription from the take, accept the preview, or delete the take to remove that audio.

Cleanup is unavailable

Cleaned Up transcripts depend on the on-device language model being available and enabled. Your Verbatim transcript remains available when cleanup cannot run.

A take stopped when I left the app

Voice Takes does not record indefinitely in the background. Moving the app to the background or locking the phone stops the active capture and saves enough recovery information to finish the transcript when possible.
