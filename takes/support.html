Support

Help with Takes.

Recording does not start

Open Settings on your iPhone and confirm that Takes has permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. The first transcription may also require Apple to download a language asset.

Cleanup is unavailable

Light and Formatted cleanup depend on the on-device language model being available and enabled. Your verbatim transcript remains available when cleanup cannot run.

A take stopped when I left the app

Takes does not record indefinitely in the background. Moving the app to the background or locking the phone stops the active capture and saves enough recovery information to finish the transcript when possible.

Contact

Email matthew.l.reed@gmail.com with a description of what happened, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. Do not send a private transcript unless you want it included in the support request.
