Help with Voice Takes.

Recording does not start

Open Settings on your iPhone and confirm that Voice Takes has permission to use the microphone. The first high-accuracy transcription may need to download the Parakeet speech model (about 600 MB); the Apple fallback may also need a language asset.

A transcript is marked provisional

This means the complete-file transcription did not finish and Voice Takes saved its live preview instead of pretending it was final. The protected recovery audio is still on the phone. Retry transcription from the take, accept the preview, or delete the take to remove that audio.

Spoken commands

In the Cleaned Up style, say these while you dictate: “new paragraph”, “new line”, “period” (or “full stop”), “comma”, “question mark”, “exclamation point”, “colon”, and “scratch that” to delete the sentence you just said. “Period” and “colon” stay as words when they’re part of a phrase, such as “the trial period”. Verbatim always keeps exactly what you said. The list is also in Settings › Spoken Commands.

A take stopped when I left the app

Voice Takes does not record indefinitely in the background. Moving the app to the background or locking the phone stops the active capture and saves enough recovery information to finish the transcript when possible.
