Privacy Policy

Voice Takes · last updated August 2026

Voice Takes has no account, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated server. The developer does not receive your recordings, transcripts, notes, personal dictionary, or information about how you use the app.

Speech transcription is performed on your iPhone using the open-source WhisperKit software and Apple system speech frameworks. Cleanup and title generation use Apple system frameworks when available. WhisperKit downloads speech-model weights, and Apple may download system-managed speech or language-model assets. These model downloads do not include your recordings, transcripts, notes, or personal dictionary. Voice Takes does not upload that content to a developer-operated service.

While you record, Voice Takes writes audio to a protected temporary recovery file so an interrupted take can be finalized safely. The file is deleted after a complete raw transcript and note metadata are stored. If finalization fails, a live transcript may be marked provisional and the audio is retained until transcription succeeds or you accept or delete that provisional take. Saved notes do not contain an audio-file reference.

Your saved transcripts, generated titles, cleanup variants, settings, and personal dictionary remain in the app's local storage until you delete them or remove the app.

Copy and Share are explicit actions. When you copy a transcript, Voice Takes writes that text to the system clipboard. When you share, the destination you choose receives the shared text and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

System features such as Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, Control Center, Lock Screen controls, and the Action Button are provided by Apple and remain subject to Apple's settings and privacy practices.
