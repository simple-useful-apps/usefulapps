Privacy Policy

Voice Takes · last updated July 2026

Voice Takes has no account, advertising, analytics, third-party SDKs, or app-owned server. The developer does not receive your recordings, transcripts, notes, personal dictionary, or information about how you use the app.

Speech transcription, cleanup, and title generation are performed on your iPhone using Apple system frameworks when those features are available. Apple may download system-managed speech or language-model assets to the device. Voice Takes does not upload your voice or transcript to a developer-operated service.

While you record, Voice Takes writes audio to a protected temporary recovery file so an interrupted take can be finalized safely. The file is deleted after the raw transcript and note metadata are stored. If finalization fails, it is retained only until recovery is completed or abandoned. Saved notes do not contain audio.

Your saved transcripts, generated titles, cleanup variants, settings, and personal dictionary remain in the app's local storage until you delete them or remove the app.

Copy and Share are explicit actions. When you copy a transcript, Voice Takes writes that text to the system clipboard. When you share, the destination you choose receives the shared text and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

System features such as Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, Control Center, Lock Screen controls, and the Action Button are provided by Apple and remain subject to Apple's settings and privacy practices.
