Privacy Policy

Chatfolio · draft for review · July 2026

Chatfolio has no account, advertising, analytics, third-party SDKs, or app-owned server. The developer does not receive your source files, questions, answers, chat history, filenames, or information about how you use the app.

Your source library and the search data derived from it are stored in your iCloud Drive so your iPhone and Mac can use the same library. Apple handles iCloud storage and synchronization under Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see the contents of your iCloud container.

Search, passage retrieval, answer generation, title generation, and citation checking are performed on your device. Chat history is stored locally on the device where the conversation took place and is not synchronized in version 1.

Chatfolio may download language-model files through an application distribution service. A model download does not include your source content, questions, answers, filenames, or chat history. Model files are stored on the device and are not backed up to iCloud.

When you copy a conversation, Chatfolio writes that text to the system clipboard. The app does not receive information about where you paste it.

Deleting a source removes it from the shared iCloud library. Deleting a chat removes it only from that device. Removing the app deletes its local chats and downloaded model files; source files remain subject to the state of your iCloud library.
