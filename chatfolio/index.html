Chatfolio

Ask your own library.

App Store for iPhone Mac App Store

Chatfolio is a pair of apps — iPhone and Mac — for asking questions of the books and documents you keep. Add an EPUB, Markdown file, web document, or plain-text file, then ask in your own words.

Every answer points back to the passage it used. Tap a citation to see the quote, then open the source with the exact words highlighted.

How it works

  • Your sources live in iCloud. Your existing iCloud keeps the library in step. There is no Chatfolio account or separate sync service.
  • The work happens on your device. Search and answer generation use downloaded models on your iPhone or Mac, not an app-owned server.
  • The source stays visible. Citations show the quoted passage and lead back to the surrounding document.

What it does

  • EPUB, Markdown, HTML, and plain text both apps
  • Scoped questions across one source or the whole library
  • Quoted citations and a focused source reader
  • Drag-and-drop importing and a three-column workspace Mac
  • Files-based importing and reading on the go iPhone
  • No Chatfolio account, advertising, analytics, or app-owned server

Chatfolio is deliberately small. It helps you ask, check, and keep reading. It does not turn your library into a feed, a social network, or another place that wants your attention.

Chatfolio is in development.
